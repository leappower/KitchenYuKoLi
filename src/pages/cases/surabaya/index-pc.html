Customer Stories

Operating a central kitchen in Surabaya serving 500+ daily meals. Traditional methods required 18 staff manually prepping and cooking in high-heat environments, with 8% waste rate due to human error.

Transformation began with YuKoLi's commercial kitchen automation team. Customized wok stations and precision seasoning systems were developed for Indonesian cuisine. After equipment calibration and staff training, the kitchen successfully transitioned to automated production.

Automated prep replaced manual labor, reducing waste from 8% to 1.2%. Staff reduced from 18 to 8, saving IDR 24 million monthly with 8.5-month payback.

Recipes and cooking parameters are securely "locked" in the system. Consistent output quality reduced customer complaints to near zero, freeing management to focus on menu R&D and expansion.
