Customer Story

In Surabaya, Indonesia, where fire and spices weave rich local flavors, a central kitchen serving traditional Indonesian dishes ran at full capacity daily, producing 800 meals. But in the traditional model, 18 employees worked in punishing heat. Manual cutting and experience-based cooking led to wildly inconsistent output. Waste rates from imprecise manual work were alarmingly high.

The operator knew that simply adding more people could no longer support business standardized expansion. The breakthrough came from working with the YuKoLi team, who mapped the kitchen workflow and combined automated woks with precise seasoning systems perfectly suited to Indonesian cooking. After a short, efficient deployment, the kitchen was transformed.

The results were immediate. YuKoLi’s precise systems took over temperature control, with standardized workflows replacing heavy manual prep. The 8% waste rate dropped to 1.2%. Kitchen staff was cut by 56%, saving the kitchen 24 million IDR (IDR 24M) monthly. "Food waste has dropped dramatically. The monthly ingredient savings alone are substantial," the kitchen manager said.

This was more than a numbers game — it was competitive rebirth. Core recipes and heat settings were now safely locked in the system, delivering quality as reliable as clockwork. Customer complaints virtually disappeared. The automation investment paid for itself in just 8.5 months, fully recovered. Management finally shifted focus from daily firefighting to new menu R&D and business expansion.p>

In an era where every hour of wasted labor drags your business down and every gram of wasted ingredients eats into your margins, Surabaya’s operators have already made the leap — cutting costs, boosting efficiency, and reshaping their bottom line.
