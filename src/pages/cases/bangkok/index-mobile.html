Customer Stories

Expanding to 8 locations required separate kitchen teams, multiplying management challenges. With Bangkok's high staff turnover, signature dish flavors fluctuated with chef departures. Lengthy new store training cycles and inconsistent quality threatened brand reputation.

YuKoLi transformed our kitchens by automating complex sauce preparation and seasoning processes. Our smart cooking system digitized the elusive "wok hei" and flavor ratios into precise parameters, with modules customized for Thai tastes to preserve signature flavors.

Kitchen staff reduced from 24 to 12, cutting labor costs by 50% and saving 270,000 THB monthly. In Bangkok's competitive F&B market, this automation investment paid for itself in just 11.3 months.

Beyond cost savings, the strategic value lies in absolute flavor standardization. Consistent taste across all 8 locations strengthens brand loyalty and enables scalable expansion.
