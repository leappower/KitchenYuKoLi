Customer Stories

Jakarta central kitchen serves 6 outlets with 1000+ daily meals. 12 chefs worked in high-temperature conditions, causing major flavor inconsistencies in signature dishes that triggered customer complaints.

With YuKoLi, we customized cooking processes for Southeast Asian stir-fry cuisine. Smart equipment digitized wok techniques, seasoning ratios, and cooking sequences previously reliant on master chefs.

Maintaining 1000-meal daily output, kitchen staff reduced from 12 to 5, saving 22 million IDR monthly with 8.0-month ROI.
