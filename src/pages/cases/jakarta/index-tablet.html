Customer Story

In Jakarta’s rapidly growing food scene, a central kitchen serving multiple locations faced a crisis hidden behind success. Daily demand was climbing, but so was kitchen chaos. High turnover, inconsistent quality, and ballooning labor costs were silently undermining the entire operation.

Before the transformation, this central kitchen was producing 600 meals daily. To barely maintain output and quality, the kitchen was packed with 12 kitchen staff. With Jakarta’s high labor costs and operational expenses, the payroll became a crushing burden. Even worse, the same dish tasted different depending on which chef cooked it — spice levels, saltiness, and doneness all varied by shift.

The turning point came with YuKoLi’s commercial kitchen solution. The team customized standardized cooking workflows for Southeast Asian cuisine — heavy on sauces and stir-frying. The smart equipment converted the wok master’s intuitive heat control and seasoning ratios into precise digital programs. Laborious manual work was replaced by machines, freeing the core chefs to become "flavor architects" focused on quality monitoring and new recipe development.p class="text-base leading-relaxed text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 mb-5" data-i18n="cases_jakarta_story_p4" >The results were immediate. While maintaining 600 meals daily, kitchen staff dropped from 12 to 5 — a 58% reduction. This saved the brand 22 million IDR (IDR 22M) monthly. Thanks to the efficiency gains, the entire equipment investment paid for itself in just 8.0 months.

What excited the team even more than the financial numbers was finally cracking the standardization core challenge. Whether rendang beef or satay, all 6 locations now delivered identical flavors. "Every store used to taste different and customers complained. Now all 6 taste identical — repeat customers have clearly increased."
