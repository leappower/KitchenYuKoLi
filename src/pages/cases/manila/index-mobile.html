Customer Story

In the Philippines, where food delivery apps fuel a booming fast-food scene, one small Manila eatery was trapped in a vicious cycle. The owner struggled daily to balance rising costs, inconsistent quality, and kitchen chaos.

To barely handle 320 daily meals, the cramped kitchen had to keep 3 chefs. They worked in shifts, sweating over the stove. Labor costs were high, and whenever someone called in sick or quit, service speed dropped and quality suffered. The owner was stuck in a cycle of hiring, training, losing staff, and hiring again.

The turning point came with YuKoLi’s commercial cooking automation. At first, the owner doubted whether a machine could replicate the complex heat control of local Filipino dishes. But the YuKoLi team customized the setup for the Philippines’ fast-paced kitchen environment and cramped space. The smart cooking station precisely controlled ingredient timing and temperature curves. What used to take three chefs working in shifts was now handled by one machine.

Kitchen operations were completely transformed. Staff was cut from 3 to 1, with labor costs dropping by 67%. The remaining staff member shifted from heavy laborer to a relaxed production manager. The financial impact was immediate: in just 5.2 months, the equipment paid for itself — all through saved labor. A true Fast Payback./p>

After installation, the fast-food joint’s daily output held steady at 320 meals, but service was noticeably faster than the all-manual era — peak-hour wait times dropped significantly. “For 3 years I worried about finding a stable wok chef. Now one person handles everything, and service is faster,” the owner said with relief. He finally freed himself from kitchen stress to focus on customer service and expansion.

When labor shortages and quality swings become the new normal in F&B, breaking free often means thinking differently. When technology can balance efficiency and flavor, the growth bottleneck may just be one decision away.
