Customer Story

In Hanoi’s Old Quarter, where the air carries notes of star anise and lime, a small eatery faced the classic dilemma: nearly crushed by its own success. With tourists flooding in, two kitchen staff ran from morning till night in a cramped, sweltering space. Peak hours meant chaos, and hand-cooking fatigue led to inconsistent broth. Daily output was stuck at 180 meals, while soaring labor costs and quality issues became growth bottlenecks./p>

The turning point came with YuKoLi’s commercial kitchen solution. Its compact modular design fit perfectly into the tiny kitchen, with installation and calibration taking just half a day. The smart cooking station took over the most labor-intensive core tasks: precise temperature control for Pho broth and standardized preparation of Banh Mi fillings, all executed with exceptional precision.p class="text-lg leading-relaxed text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 mb-5" data-i18n="cases_hanoi_story_p3">The change was immediate. What once required two people’s full attention was now handled by a single operator. Kitchen staff was cut from 2 to 1, achieving a 50% reduction. This saved the shop 8 million VND (VND 8M) monthly, with equipment ROI in just 5.1 months. Daily output stabilized at 180 meals with consistent taste and faster service.

For Mr. Chen, the biggest gain was achieving true standardization. Precise temperature control and automated portioning ended the feast-or-famine quality swings. Every bowl of Pho and Banh Mi was now as consistent as a photocopy. “Pho and Banh Mi both work perfectly. Even foreign tourists praise the taste,” he said, finally understanding: machines guard quality so he can preserve culinary soul.>
