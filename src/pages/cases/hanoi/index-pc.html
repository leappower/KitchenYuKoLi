Customer Stories

A Hanoi street stall serving 150+ daily meals faced quality inconsistencies and high labor costs. Each bowl depended on chef skills—quality dropped when the head chef was absent.

YuKoLi's compact design fit the tiny kitchen, installed in half a day. The smart station automated critical processes—from precise broth control to standardized Bánh Mì fillings.
