Group Catering Pain Points

Four core challenges in centralized catering

Time-sensitive pressure

Lunch rush (11:30-13:00) requires thousands of meals in 1.5 hours. Manual operations lead to long queues, shortages, and complaints.

Limited menu variety

Limited to 4-6 daily dishes due to staffing constraints, resulting in low satisfaction and repeat rates.

Food safety non-negotiable

Critical for institutional cafeterias. Manual processes struggle with consistent temperature and hygiene control.

Labor-intensive operations

Traditional kitchens need 8-15 staff with rising recruitment/training costs and high turnover.

High Energy Costs

Traditional gas stoves consume massive energy – central kitchens often spend thousands monthly on gas. Energy costs remain a persistent pain point.

Severe Food Waste

Manual portioning is inaccurate – requiring 15–20% extra prep per meal to prevent shortages, resulting in significant food waste.
