Canteen Pain Points

Six core challenges in centralized meal service

High-Pressure Time Windows

Lunch peak concentrates at 11:30–13:00 — thousands of meals needed in 1.5 hours. Manual operations often lead to long queues, shortages, and complaints.

Limited Menu Variety

Limited by staff and equipment, only 4–6 dishes can be offered daily. Long rotation cycles lead to low satisfaction and declining return rates.

Food Safety Is Non-Negotiable

Food safety incidents in institutional catering have massive impact, especially in schools. Manual operations struggle to guarantee temperature and hygiene consistency.

Labor-Intensive

Traditional canteens need 8–15 chefs + helpers. Labor costs keep rising. High chef turnover means constant recruitment and training costs.

High Energy Costs

Traditional gas ranges consume heavily — monthly gas bills for central kitchens often exceed thousands. Energy costs are a persistent headache.

Significant Food Waste

Manual portioning is imprecise — 15–20% extra is prepared each meal to prevent shortages, resulting in massive food waste.
