Common Small F&B Pain Points

The hardest part isn't opening - but maintaining kitchen efficiency, consistent flavors, and reliable speed.

High Chef Costs & Turnover

Skilled Southeast Asian chefs demand high salaries - and trained staff often leave. One chef's absence paralyzes the kitchen, causing order backlogs, customer waits, and bad reviews.

Cramped Kitchen Layout

10-15㎡ kitchens must accommodate prep tables, stoves, and packing areas. Traditional gas stoves turn kitchens into ovens in summer - unbearable for staff.

Chef Rotation = Flavor Changes

Regulars come for signature flavors. Different chefs mean inconsistent taste - customers ask"Did you change chefs?" and stop returning.

Peak Hour Bottleneck

11:30 AM rush with dine-in and delivery orders. Two woks can't keep up. 20-minute waits lead to next-day cancellations and bad reviews.

Oven-like Kitchen, Huge Energy Bills?

Open-flame kitchens exceed 40℃ in summer. Staff last 2 hours max. Monthly gas cylinder changes and rising electricity bills eat profits.

Equipment Failure = Business Shutdown?

Noon stove failure means no cooking. Suppliers need 3-5 days for repairs - how to operate for 3 days?
