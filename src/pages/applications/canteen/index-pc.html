What Equipment Does a Canteen Need?

Whether you survive the lunch rush without running out of food or creating long queues depends on three things: how fast the wok cooks, whether you can steam enough rice at once, and whether the serving counter holds temperature. Dishwashing efficiency determines how quickly the kitchen closes.

Common Questions from Canteen Managers

Get your questions answered before placing an order.

Can canteen equipment handle small-scale service? expand_more
Yes. Equipment comes in different capacities — from school canteens serving hundreds to corporate canteens serving thousands. Share your capacity and menu size for a tailored proposal.
Can equipment maintain food temperature? expand_more
Yes. Equipment supports constant-temperature holding. After cooking, dishes can be kept warm in holding mode — no one gets a cold meal.
Can someone with no chef experience operate the equipment? expand_more
Yes. Recipes have preset heat and stir-fry timing — just select the dish, add ingredients, and press start. New operators are trained and independent in 30 minutes.
What about warranty and maintenance? expand_more
YuKoLi offers a 1-year whole-machine warranty and 3-year warranty on core components (IGBT, coils, wok body). Years of iteration have reinforced all wear points. Proprietary quick-release design ships with extra spare parts. Non-human-caused damage gets a 24-hour response from the local after-sales team with dispatch from the nearest warehouse.
Can it handle halal or other special dietary requirements? expand_more
Yes. Equipment doesn't involve cross-contamination between ingredients. Different dishes on the same unit only require simple cleaning. Custom recipes can store cooking parameters for special dietary needs.
Is electricity consumption high? Will canteen power supply handle it? expand_more
YuKoLi commercial induction and gas equipment both use leading energy-saving technology. Induction units achieve 90%+ thermal efficiency, and energy-efficient gas models significantly reduce consumption. The optimal solution depends on your electrical capacity, ventilation conditions, and local energy prices — contact sales for a customized energy assessment.
