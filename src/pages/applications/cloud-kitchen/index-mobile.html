Extremely Low Space Utilization

Cloud kitchen rents are charged by area, but traditional ranges occupy 60% of the space. Only half of an 80 m² kitchen is actually usable for cooking — effective cooking area may be just 35 m².

speed

Serving Speed Bottleneck

Lunch peak orders are 5–8x normal volume. Manual cooking speed can't keep up. Once the late delivery rate exceeds 15%, platforms downgrade your ranking — traffic drops off a cliff.

device_hub

Multi-Brand Equipment Sharing Conflicts

One site running 3–5 brands means frequent equipment scheduling conflicts. Switching and cleaning takes time, and cross-flavor contamination between cuisines is a real risk.

local_fire_department

Emissions & Fire Safety Compliance

Cloud kitchens in commercial complexes and residential buildings face strict grease emission and fire safety requirements. Retrofitting traditional open-flame equipment for compliance is prohibitively expensive.
