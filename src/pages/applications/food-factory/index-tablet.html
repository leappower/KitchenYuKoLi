Deep Challenges Facing the Food Factory Industry

HACCP compliance pressure, batch traceability challenges, 24-hour line stability, rising energy costs — these pain points are slowing down food factory profitability and expansion.

gavel

HACCP/ISO Compliance Barriers

Food factories must pass HACCP, GMP, and FSSC22000 certifications. Multiple manual handling steps make critical control points (CCPs) hard to sustain — audit failure means production halt or fines.

qr_code_scanner

Missing Batch Traceability

Food safety incidents require factories to complete batch traceability within 2 hours. Paper records or manual entry are error-prone and slow — making it impossible to quickly locate affected batches.

schedule

24-Hour Line Instability

Large-scale food production typically requires 3-shift operation. Manual handling fatigue is high, night shift quality drops noticeably. Equipment failures lack early warning — a single stoppage can cost tens of thousands.

bolt

Rising Energy Costs

Energy consumption (gas, steam, electricity) accounts for a significant portion of operating costs. Many factories still use aging, inefficient heating equipment without scientific energy monitoring — resources are wasted.
