Unstable Production

Manual operations cause significant batch-to-batch variations in output and quality, making it hard to meet order deadlines

Food Safety Compliance Pressure

Stringent HACCP/GMP/FSSC22000 certification requirements with tightening regulations

Low Standardization

Downtime from Equipment Failure
