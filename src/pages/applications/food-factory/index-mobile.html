Key Challenges in Food Manufacturing

HACCP compliance, traceability, 24/7 stability, energy costs - these challenges hinder profitability and growth.

gavel

High Compliance Barriers

Food factories must comply with HACCP, GMP, FSSC22000 certifications. Traditional manual processes struggle with CCP compliance, risking shutdowns or fines.

qr_code_scanner

Lacking Traceability

Food safety incidents require batch tracing within 2 hours. Paper/manual records are inefficient and error-prone, delaying problem resolution.

schedule

Unstable Production

Round-the-clock production suffers from operator fatigue and quality drops. Equipment failures without alerts can cause $10k+ losses per incident.

bolt

Soaring Energy Costs

Energy costs (gas, steam, electricity) dominate operational expenses. Outdated equipment and poor monitoring waste resources amid rising prices.
