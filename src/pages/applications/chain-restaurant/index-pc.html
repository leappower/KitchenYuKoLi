Core Challenges for Chain Restaurants

From output consistency to rapid expansion — every step is a test.

Inconsistent Output

Highly dependent on individual chef skill — different stores produce different flavors, damaging brand reputation and repeat visits.

Expansion Bottleneck

Standardization is hard to replicate in new stores. Each new opening requires rebuilding a chef team — 3–6 months to achieve stable output.

High Labor Costs

Chef salaries 8,000–15,000 CNY/month. Hard to recruit, long training cycles, high turnover. Labor costs consume 25–35% of revenue.

Compliance Pressure

Oil emission, fire safety, and food hygiene regulations are getting stricter. Manual operations struggle to guarantee compliance for every meal.

Supply Chain Complexity

Unified ingredient procurement and distribution across multiple stores is complex. Stockout risks frequently disrupt normal operations.

Equipment Maintenance Costs

High maintenance costs for commercial kitchen equipment. Sudden breakdowns cause business interruption — slow repair response compounds cost pressure.
