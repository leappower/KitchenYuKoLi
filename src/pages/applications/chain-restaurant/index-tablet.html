Chain Restaurant FAQs

Clarify all questions before ordering to avoid pitfalls.

How soon can new stores meet output standards?

expand_more
Plug-and-play operation with preset recipes - no professional chefs needed. New stores can serve meals on opening day.

Do existing stores need to close for equipment upgrades?

expand_more
No. Countertop units allow gradual replacement without business interruption.

How to manage recipes across chains?

expand_more
Recipes can be imported/exported. HQ-developed new dishes can be deployed to all stores simultaneously.

Warranty coverage? Cross-region service?

expand_more
YuKoLi offers 1-year full machine warranty, 3-year core parts. Products feature reinforced wear points and modular design. Each shipment includes spare wear parts. Non-human damage gets free on-site repair by local teams, supported by 10 warehouses for fast response.

How many units does a new store need?

expand_more
Depends on menu and output. Typically 2-3 units cover core dishes for fast food chains. Bring your menu for customized advice.

Can equipment make hotpot base?

expand_more
Yes. Both braising and wok machines can standardize hotpot base preparation for consistent flavor.
