Frying Equipment FAQ

What foods can be fried? expand_more
Common fried items including fried chicken, fish, fries, shrimp, and meatballs. Different ingredients require different temperature and time settings — the equipment supports multi-group presets.
How often should the oil be changed? expand_more
The auto filtration system extends oil life by 30% compared to traditional frying. Actual change intervals depend on usage frequency and ingredients. The device prompts when it's time.
What safety features are included? expand_more
Auto shutoff on overheating, boil-dry protection, oil temperature alarm, and auto fire suppression (select models). Multiple safety layers compliant with commercial kitchen standards.
How many servings per batch? expand_more
Capacity varies by model — typically 5–20 servings per batch. For high-volume output, choose a large-capacity dual-tank model.
