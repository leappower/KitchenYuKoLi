Stewing Equipment FAQ

What types of soup can be stewed? expand_more
Slow-cooked broth, herbal soup, bone broth, mushroom soup, vegetarian soup, and more. Supports custom temperature and time settings for different recipes.
How does it compare to open-flame stewing? expand_more
Electromagnetic precision temp control delivers far more stable results than open flame. In blind taste tests, most tasters find electromagnetic-stewed broth richer with less batch-to-batch variation.
Does it support auto keep-warm? expand_more
Yes. After stewing completes, it automatically switches to keep-warm mode (adjustable, typically 60–80°C) to maintain broth temperature for on-demand serving.
What capacity is suitable? expand_more
Models range from small canteens (50L/day) to central kitchens (500+ L/day). Large-capacity models are ideal for centralized prep with branch distribution.
