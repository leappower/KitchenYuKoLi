Can't Keep Up at Breakfast

Traditional steamers hold only a few layers. Hundreds of buns require multiple rounds, and customers wait too long.

Condensation Dripping

Traditional steamers drip heavily, leaving buns soggy and unappetizing — appearance and texture both suffer.

Hard to Control Timing

Different ingredients need different steaming times. Multi-item steaming often leads to overcooking or undercooking.

High Energy Costs

Traditional steaming has low thermal efficiency. Lots of steam is wasted, and gas/electricity bills stay high.
