Cutting Equipment FAQ

What ingredients can the cutting machine process? expand_more
Vegetables, root crops, and hard ingredients. Commonly supports: potato, carrot, cabbage, onion, cucumber, celery, and more. For special ingredients, we recommend sending samples for testing.
Can I adjust the cut specifications? expand_more
Yes. Shred thickness, slice thickness, and dice size can be controlled by changing blades or adjusting parameters. Multiple specifications are typically available.
Is cleaning easy? expand_more
Quick-release design allows blades and feed chutes to be removed for fast cleaning. Daily cleaning takes 5–10 minutes.
What capacity kitchens is this suitable for? expand_more
Models are available for small restaurants (50 kg/day) to central kitchens (500+ kg/day). We recommend sizing based on daily prep volume.
