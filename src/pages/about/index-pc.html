Our Story

Founded in 2003 in Foshan, China's kitchenware manufacturing hub, YuKoLi started as a small workshop with one mission: transforming commercial kitchens through smart automation. After 23 years of technological innovation, we've grown into a full-cycle manufacturer of intelligent kitchen equipment.

Today, our 200+ smart devices serve clients in 68+ countries – from 5-star hotel kitchens in Bangkok to QSR chains in Ho Chi Minh City, from corporate canteens in Kuala Lumpur to institutional meal programs producing 30,000 servings daily. Whether you're a chain brand or regional distributor, YuKoLi powers your standardized expansion.
