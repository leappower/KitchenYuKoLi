FAQ

What is the warranty period? expand_more
All YuKoLi equipment comes with a 1-year warranty starting from the date of acceptance. During the warranty period, we fully cover all repair costs resulting from quality issues (excluding human damage, improper use, and consumable parts).
What is not covered under the warranty? expand_more
The following are not covered: human damage, improper use (e.g., overload), natural disasters (e.g., flood, fire), failure to operate/maintain per manual, unauthorized modification, or use of third-party parts. For questions regarding warranty scope, please confirm via WhatsApp.
How are repair costs calculated outside the warranty period? expand_more
Repairs outside the warranty period follow a transparent quotation system. Engineers provide a detailed quote (parts + labor) after inspection, and work only begins upon customer confirmation. There are no hidden fees. If repair costs are too high, we will recommend replacement options for your reference.
How to apply for warranty extension? expand_more
We recommend contacting us 30 days before the standard warranty expires to apply for an extension. Our after-sales team will provide customized extension plans and discounted quotes based on equipment model and usage. Early renewal typically enjoys a 10-15% price discount.
Can parts still be purchased if the equipment is discontinued? expand_more
Yes. YuKoLi commits to supplying spare parts for discontinued models for no less than 5 years, ensuring your equipment receives original parts support throughout its entire lifecycle.
How long does it take to resolve a reported fault? expand_more
We respond based on fault severity: Emergency faults within 1 hour, serious faults within 4 hours, general faults within 24 hours, and minor faults within 48 hours. Additionally, over 60% of faults can be resolved quickly via remote diagnosis without waiting for an engineer visit.
