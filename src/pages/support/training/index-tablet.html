Frequently Asked Questions

Is equipment training free? expand_more
Basic operation training after installation and delivery is free and included in the installation service. Advanced training (intermediate/advanced courses) and customized programs are quoted based on content and duration. Contact us for detailed proposals.
What languages are available for training? expand_more
We currently offer training materials and on-site training in Chinese, English, and Thai. For other languages (Vietnamese, Malay, etc.), please contact us in advance and we will arrange translation support. Online courses are primarily available in Chinese and English.
How long does training take? expand_more
The beginner course (basic operations) takes approximately 4 hours, typically completed on the installation day. The intermediate course takes approximately 5.5 hours and the advanced course approximately 7 hours. Sessions can be scheduled in batches to suit your availability. An operation assessment is provided after training.
How do I get operation manuals? expand_more
Each unit is delivered with a printed operation manual. Digital versions are available in the "Manual Downloads" section on this page. For manuals of other equipment or translation requests, please contact us via WhatsApp or Email.
