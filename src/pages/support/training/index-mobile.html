FAQ

Is equipment training free? expand_more
Basic operation training after equipment installation and delivery is free and included in the installation service. Advanced training (intermediate/advanced courses) and customized training plans are quoted based on content and duration. For advanced training, please contact us for a detailed proposal.
What languages are available for training? expand_more
We currently provide training materials and on-site training services in Chinese, English, and Thai. If other languages are needed (Vietnamese, Malay, etc.), please communicate in advance, and we will arrange translation support. Online courses are mainly available in Chinese and English versions.
How long does the training take? expand_more
The introductory course (basic operation) takes about 4 hours, usually completed on the day of installation acceptance. The intermediate course takes about 5.5 hours, and the advanced course about 7 hours. These can be conducted in batches according to the client's schedule.
How to obtain the operation manual? expand_more
Each equipment comes with a paper operation manual upon delivery. The electronic version can be obtained in the "Manual Download" area of this page. If you need operation manuals for other equipment or have translation requirements, please contact us via WhatsApp or Email.
