FAQs

Is training free? expand_more
Basic training post-installation is free. Advanced/custom training is quoted based on content and duration. Contact us for details.
Available languages? expand_more
Available in Chinese, English, and Thai. Other languages (Vietnamese, Malay) require advance notice for translation support.
Training duration? expand_more
Beginner: 4hrs (installation day). Intermediate: 5.5hrs. Advanced: 7hrs. Can be scheduled in sessions with certification.
How to get manuals? expand_more
Paper manuals come with equipment. Digital versions available under "Manual Download". Contact via WhatsApp/Email for others.
