Frequently Asked Questions

From equipment selection to after-sales coverage — four key categories covering your most common concerns.

How do I choose the right commercial kitchen equipment? expand_more
Consider these factors: 1) Cuisine type — induction wok ranges for Chinese stir-fry, ovens and fryers for Western baking, food processors for fast-food prep; 2) Daily output — estimate capacity based on daily covers; 3) Kitchen space — countertop units save space, floor-standing units offer higher capacity; 4) Power supply — confirm whether your site has 220V or 380V. Submit your requirements via our "Get a Quote" page and our engineers will recommend the optimal solution for your specific needs.
What is the difference between 220V and 380V equipment? How do I choose? expand_more
220V (single-phase) suits small restaurants, cafés, and food stalls — plugs directly into standard mains power, simple installation. 380V (three-phase) suits large commercial kitchens, central kitchens, and canteens with higher capacity demands — greater power, faster heating, higher efficiency, but requires a certified electrician for wiring. Recommendation: check your kitchen's power supply before deciding. If unsure, our engineers will conduct an on-site power survey before installation.
Countertop vs. floor-standing equipment — which should I choose? expand_more
Countertop units are compact and flexible, ideal for small kitchens with limited space, with relatively lower daily output and more affordable pricing. Floor-standing units offer higher capacity and stability, suited for mid-to-large commercial kitchens and central kitchens, typically with larger bowls and more powerful motors. Recommendation: for kitchens under 30㎡, prioritize countertop units; above 30㎡, choose floor-standing based on output requirements. We provide complimentary layout planning for full-line configurations.
Can equipment be customized? Do you support OEM/ODM? expand_more
Yes. We offer OEM badging and ODM co-development partnerships. Customization covers equipment appearance, functional configuration, dimensions, and control systems. Custom projects typically require 30–60 working days, depending on complexity. Contact our sales team via WhatsApp or email to discuss your customization requirements.

What should I pay attention to when first using the equipment? expand_more
Follow these steps for first use: 1) Inspect for shipping damage inside and out; 2) Verify that the power supply matches the equipment nameplate before powering on; 3) Run empty for 5–10 minutes on first power-up to check for normal operation; 4) Familiarize yourself with controls and parameter settings per the user manual; 5) Clean the interior thoroughly before first use. Our engineers provide on-site training during installation and commissioning to ensure your staff can operate the equipment proficiently.
Do smart devices support recipe import? How does it work? expand_more
Yes. YuKoLi smart devices support custom recipe import via USB or Wi-Fi. Create recipes on the control panel by setting temperature, time, stirring speed, and other parameters, then save as preset programs. We also provide a built-in recipe library covering Chinese stir-fry, Western baking, Southeast Asian cuisine, and more. For batch imports, contact technical support to obtain the recipe management tool.
How long can the equipment run continuously? expand_more
YuKoLi commercial equipment is designed for 24/7 continuous operation. We recommend a brief rest and cleaning every 8–12 hours to maintain optimal performance and extend equipment lifespan. Built-in overheat protection automatically shuts down the equipment when temperatures exceed safe limits; it can be restarted after cooling.
How do I clean and maintain the equipment? expand_more
Daily cleaning: Wipe surfaces with a damp cloth after each use; use food-grade cleaners for interior residue. Daily checks: Clean filters and drains; inspect blades and transmission components. Weekly maintenance: Lubricate moving parts with food-grade oil; check power cables. Monthly deep clean: Fully disassemble and wash interior components; check wear on consumable parts; perform functional tests. Refer to the maintenance section of your equipment's user manual for detailed maintenance schedules.

What should I do if the equipment fails to start or shuts down during operation? expand_more
Won't start: 1) Check power connection; 2) Verify main switch and equipment switch; 3) Check for error codes on display. Shuts down during operation: 1) Overheat protection may have triggered — wait 10 minutes to cool, then restart; 2) Check for low water/oil; 3) Inspect power cables for looseness. If unresolved after these checks, contact after-sales immediately via WhatsApp with photos of the equipment nameplate and any error codes — our engineers will follow up promptly.
