FAQ

What do I need to prepare before installation? expand_more
Please ensure the installation site has basic power, water supply/drainage, and ventilation. Each unit is fully tested and calibrated before shipment — ready to use upon arrival. Our installation service mainly helps you plan placement and complete connections. We provide an environment checklist in advance and confirm everything during the survey.
How long does installation take? expand_more
A single unit typically takes half a day to install. A full production line (3+ units) usually takes 1–2 days. Equipment is fully tested at the factory before shipping — no additional calibration time needed after installation.
Who handles the installation? expand_more
You can choose our certified engineers for on-site installation, or install it yourself — every unit is fully tested and calibrated before shipping, so it's ready to use right out of the box. If you have an electrician, self-installation is straightforward.
How do I verify the installation? expand_more
With engineer installation, they will power on and verify everything works, then provide a report, manual, and train your staff on-site. With self-installation, simply confirm the unit arrived in good condition and power it on. Either way, every unit is fully tested before leaving the factory.
How much does installation cost? expand_more
Installation fees cover engineer travel and standard parts. We provide a free quote first — no work begins until you approve. No hidden charges.
Do I need to close my restaurant during installation? expand_more
A single unit installation typically takes just a few hours with minimal business impact. For full production line installations, we schedule in advance and try to work during non-business hours to minimize disruption.
How long is the warranty after installation? expand_more
1-year whole-unit warranty (from acceptance date), 3-year warranty for core components (IGBT modules, induction coils, pot body), 12-month warranty for control boards (excl. non-quality issues). Free replacement for quality-related defects during warranty period.
What if the equipment breaks down? expand_more
Report via WhatsApp/phone/email. Emergency faults responded to within 2 hours. 60%+ of issues can be resolved remotely.
