What Conditions Does Your Kitchen Require

These are the basic conditions for normal equipment operation. Unsure? Our engineer will confirm during the site visit.

Requires Three-Phase Power (380V/220V)

Most commercial kitchens already meet this. Unsure? The engineer will confirm during the survey.

Water supply and drainage are required

Standard commercial kitchen sink configuration is sufficient. If water pressure is insufficient, a booster pump can be installed.

Exhaust system is required

The kitchen must be equipped with exhaust facilities. If not yet available, we can recommend partnered exhaust engineering contractors.

Certain operating space is required

Specific space requirements depend on the equipment model you choose. During the survey, engineers will measure precisely to ensure convenient operation and maintenance after installation.

FAQ

What preparations are required before equipment installation? expand_more
Please ensure the installation site meets basic requirements for power, water supply/drainage, and ventilation. Every unit undergoes complete debugging and testing before leaving the factory and is ready for use immediately upon power-up after delivery. Our installation service focuses on planning placement and completing pipeline connections. We will provide an environmental requirements checklist in advance and finalize confirmation during the site survey.
How long does it take to install a set of equipment? expand_more
A single unit typically takes half a day to install on-site. A full production line (3+ units) generally requires 1–2 days. All equipment is fully debugged and tested before shipment — simply power on after installation, no additional commissioning needed.
Who is responsible for equipment installation? expand_more
You can choose installation by a YuKoLi certified engineer or perform self-installation. Each unit is fully debugged and tested before shipment and ready for normal use upon power-up after arrival. If you have an electrician, self-installation is straightforward.
How is acceptance handled after installation? expand_more
If you choose on-site engineer installation, the engineer will power on the equipment to verify normal operation after installation, provide an acceptance report and operation manual, and conduct on-site training for your chefs. If you choose self-installation, simply confirm the equipment arrives intact and powers on normally before use. Regardless of the method, all equipment is fully debugged and tested before leaving the factory.
How are installation fees calculated? expand_more
Installation fees cover engineer on-site labor and standard accessories. We provide a free quote first and schedule the visit after confirmation. There are no hidden charges.
Do I need to close my store during installation? expand_more
Single unit installation usually takes a few hours, with minimal impact on operations. For full line installation, we will coordinate the schedule in advance and aim to arrange it during non-operating hours to minimize impact on your business.
What is the warranty period for equipment after installation? expand_more
1-year whole-unit warranty (from acceptance date). Free replacement for parts damaged due to quality issues during the warranty period.
What if the equipment malfunctions? expand_more
Report via WhatsApp / phone / email. Emergency faults responded to within 1 hour. 60%+ of issues can be resolved through remote guidance.
