Spare Parts FAQ

How to Confirm the Required Spare Part Model? expand_more
You can confirm via the following methods: 1) Check the model and serial number on the equipment nameplate; 2) Take a photo of the part to be replaced and send it to our spare parts specialist; 3) Describe the malfunction, and our engineers will help identify the required part. To avoid shipping errors, we recommend confirming before placing an order.
Can Third-Party Parts Be Used? expand_more
We strongly advise against using third-party parts. Non-original parts may differ in material, specifications, and performance, leading to reduced equipment performance, increased safety risks, and potential impact on warranty rights. YuKoLi original parts undergo strict quality testing to ensure perfect matching with the equipment.
How Long Does Spare Part Shipping Take? expand_more
Standard parts (blades, filters, seals, etc.) are in stock at Southeast Asia regional warehouses and will be shipped within 48 hours of order placement. Non-standard parts (control boards, custom components, etc.) may require 5-10 working days for allocation or production. We will inform you of the estimated delivery time in advance.
Which Common Wear Parts Are Recommended for Advance Stocking? expand_more
Based on customer usage data, we recommend stocking these consumables: vegetable cutter blades (3–6 months), oil mesh filters (clean monthly, replace every 3 months), sealing rings (replace every 6 months), and thermostats (replace annually if temperature is adjusted frequently). We provide personalized stocking recommendations based on your specific equipment model.
质保期内更换配件是否收费？ expand_more
整机保修 1 年。质保期内因质量问题损坏的配件免费更换（损耗件除外）。核心配件（IGBT 模块、电磁线圈、锅体）享 3 年质保。
控制板损坏怎么办？ expand_more
控制板保修 1 年（非质量问题除外）。如需更换，提供原厂全新配件，48 小时发货，必要时安排工程师远程或上门指导安装。
常见故障 60% 以上可远程解决，如何操作？ expand_more
通过 WhatsApp 或电话联系我们的技术团队，发送设备照片和故障描述，工程师会进行远程诊断和指导。多数常见问题无需等待配件或上门服务即可解决。
