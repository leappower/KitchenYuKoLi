Parts FAQs

How to confirm required part numbers? expand_more
Confirm via: 1) Equipment nameplate model/serial; 2) Photos of damaged parts; 3) Fault description. Verification recommended before ordering.
Can third-party parts be used? expand_more
Strongly advise against third-party parts. Non-OEM parts may compromise performance, safety, and warranty. YuKoLi parts undergo strict QC for perfect fit.
What's the parts delivery timeline? expand_more
Standard parts (blades/filters/seals) ship within 48 hours from SEA stock. Custom parts (control boards) require 5-10 working days with advance notice.
Which wear parts should be stocked in advance? expand_more
Recommended spares: Vegetable blades (3-6 months), oil filters (monthly/quarterly), seals (6 months), thermostats (annual for frequent adjustments). Customized suggestions available.
Are warranty replacements charged? expand_more
1-year full warranty. Free replacement for quality issues (excludes wear parts). Core parts (IGBT modules, coils, pots) have 3-year warranty.
What if control board fails? expand_more
1-year control board warranty (excludes non-quality issues). OEM replacements shipped in 48 hours with remote/on-site installation support.
How to resolve 60%+ common faults remotely? expand_more
Contact our tech team via WhatsApp or phone. Send device photos and fault descriptions for remote diagnosis. Most common issues are resolved without waiting for parts or on-site service.
