FAQ

How to report equipment malfunction? expand_more
You can contact our regional engineer via WhatsApp with one click, or call the service hotline. We support remote video diagnosis, and most issues can be resolved remotely within 2 hours. If on-site service is needed, we arrive within 48 hours.
What is the warranty period? expand_more
All equipment is covered by a 1-year warranty from the date of shipment (excluding consumables). Repair costs arising from quality issues during the warranty period are borne by us.
Do you support overseas service? expand_more
We have service stations in 10 Southeast Asian countries, including Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia, and the Philippines, offering localized after-sales support. Other regions are covered via remote service.
What is the lead time for spare parts supply? expand_more
Standard spare parts are dispatched from the Southeast Asia regional warehouse within 48 hours (some parts require stock reallocation). Non-standard parts can be delivered rapidly via express shipping. We recommend reviewing the manual to stock up on common wear parts in advance.
What are the qualifications of the repair engineers? expand_more
All service engineers are trained and certified by YuKoLi headquarters, proficient in equipment structure and common fault troubleshooting. The Southeast Asia local team is stationed on-site to ensure rapid response.
How are repair costs calculated? expand_more
Repairs are free of charge during the warranty period (including labor and parts). After the warranty period, engineers will provide a detailed quote after inspection, and repairs will only commence upon your confirmation. There are no hidden fees.
