Canteen FAQs

Clarify your questions before selecting equipment.

What canteen scale is suitable for your equipment?

expand_more
Our solutions cover 200-5,000 diners. Small canteens (200-500) use countertop units, medium (500-2,000) use multiple induction woks, large (2,000-5,000) use production-line setups. Share your diner count for a tailored plan.

How does the equipment handle peak lunch hours?

expand_more
Induction woks cook faster than gas, with batch cooking at full capacity. Warming stations hold food at serving temp. Multiple units in parallel handle 3,000+ diners in 90 minutes.

Does the equipment support multi-cuisine menus?

expand_more
Yes. Preset recipes cover Chinese, Western, and Southeast Asian cuisines. Switch between recipes instantly via the control panel. Custom recipes also supported.

How about food safety and temperature control?

expand_more
Real-time core temperature monitoring ensures ≥75°C sterilization. All cooking data logged for inspection. HACCP-compliant processes with full traceability.

What's the warranty and after-sales service?

expand_more
1-year full machine warranty, 3-year core component coverage. Fast replacement design with spare parts from 10+ warehouse locations nationwide. Local service teams for on-site repairs.

How much space does the equipment need?

expand_more
Countertop models fit standard kitchen counters (60cm depth). Floor models occupy 1-2㎡ each. Space planning included in our consultation – we'll work with your kitchen layout.
