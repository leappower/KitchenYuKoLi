Four Scaling Challenges

From consistency to rapid scaling—every step is a challenge

Flavor Inconsistency

Inconsistent flavors across locations and chefs damage brand reputation and customer retention.

High Training Costs

3-6 months to train chefs for new locations—slows expansion dramatically.

SOP Implementation Issues

Paper recipes rely on human execution—temp, timing, portions become guesswork. SOPs fail.

Linear Labor Cost Growth

More locations = more chefs. $1,000-$2,000 monthly salaries, hiring challenges, 25%-35% revenue spent on labor.

Supply Chain Complexity

Complex multi-location supply chains. Frequent stockouts disrupt operations.

Maintenance Costs

High equipment maintenance costs. Breakdowns cause closures—slow repairs worsen losses.
