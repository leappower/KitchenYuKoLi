Common Steaming Pain Points

Inefficiency, timing challenges, inconsistent quality—especially problematic during breakfast rush.

Insufficient breakfast rush capacity

Traditional steamers have limited layers. Making hundreds of buns requires multiple batches, causing long customer wait times.

Condensation dripping issue

Condensation dripping makes buns soggy and collapsed—ruining both appearance and texture.

Inconsistent cooking time

Different ingredients require varying steaming times. Mixed batches often result in overcooked or undercooked items.

High energy costs

Traditional steamers waste energy. Excessive steam loss leads to high gas/electricity bills.
